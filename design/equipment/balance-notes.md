SHIPPED BUDGET MODEL (locked, unchanged from the authoring ruler): every bit is bought from a fixed point budget set by rarity. Stat prices ATK/DEF/SPD = 2, EN = 1, part-HP = 0.5, core-HP = 0.4. Weight is a rebate on the bit (0.2/pt, capped at 40% of its raw stat-points) but the REAL cost is at build level: WEIGHT_BUDGET 100 free, then -1 SPD per point over (SPD clamps >=1). Ability value: SINGLE power x1.5, MULTI power x hit_count x1.0, GUARD DEF_BUFF x1.5, GUARD PART_RESTORE x1.0, minus 0.5 x mana_cost (floored at 0). Rarity bands: body COMMON 14-18 / RARE 18-22 / EPIC 22-26; core COMMON 26-30 / RARE 30-34 / RARE 30-34. Rarity buys ceiling + one signature + a named downside, NEVER raw dominance - so commons stay relevant forever and the meta stays wide.

WHAT SHIPPED: 67 bits (9 cores + 58 body). VERIFIED by script over the full keep-set: (1) every bit lands in its rarity band; (2) ZERO strict-dominance pairs within any slot+rarity (no bit is a pure upgrade of a same-rarity peer); (3) no soft-cap violations (SINGLE power <=5/7/9, MULTI hits <=3/4/5 & per-hit <=3/3/4, GUARD amount <=4/6/8); (4) healthy spread in every non-core slot - HEAD 7C/3R/1E, ARM_L 6C/3R/3E, ARM_R 6C/4R/2E, LEGS 7C/3R/1E, BACK 7C/3R/2E (arms combined 12C/7R/5E); (5) cores = a clean 3x3 grid, attack/defense/mana each x (2 COMMON + 1 RARE).

FIXES MADE during the audit of the 115 authored bits: (a) DROPPED the two under-band legs (grumble_co_bollard_legs 10.8 and sovereign_brass_legs_cornice 13.0) - both were also strictly dominated by better common legs, so they were pure filler. (b) Culled an overcrowded common-legs and common-back cluster that produced ~15 strict-dominance pairs; kept only the Pareto frontier (distinct niches: pure-speed, featherweight, balanced, tanky, mana-battery). (c) Resolved the DEF_BUFF-vs-PART_RESTORE 'dominance' artifacts (the model prices restore cheaper per point, which made restore bits look strictly worse than same-stat brace bits) by differentiating stat profiles rather than deleting the tool - e.g. re-tuned cobble_sons_arm_mendclaw to weight 10 so it wins on weight while the brace wins on ability value. (d) AUTHORED 2 new bits to fill real gaps: silksteel_head_oracle (the missing HEAD EPIC - a fragile, mana-starved core-sniper) and errant_legs_rampart (a 3rd RARE legs, armored-and-mobile, to complement the two pure-speed rares). (e) Trimmed the 22 authored cores to a curated 9 (the brief's 6-9), keeping the legacy artificer_first starter trio wired into save/kit/challengers and one COMMON + one RARE branded core per affinity. (f) Nudged boldheart_arm_meteor from mana_cost 5 -> 4 to respect the model's 'big EPIC nukes cost 3-4 mana' rule while its true throttle stays weight 50.

RESIDUAL RISKS TO WATCH IN PLAYTEST: (1) WEIGHT TAX MUST BITE. A full heavy build (e.g. Meteor w50 + a heavy core + Bedrock legs) blows past 100 by 50+, clamping SPD to 1. Confirm that being SPD-clamped genuinely feels bad (you always move last) and that WEIGHT_BUDGET=100 is the right knob with six slots - if heavy stacks don't feel slow, the weight rebate is doing too much and the whole light-vs-heavy axis collapses. The elite (Cogsworth) and boss (Brassmore) are intentionally SPD-clamped so the PLAYER acts first and can break their arms before the big swing - verify that reads as fair tension, not a free win. (2) MULTI vs SINGLE. With more high-count MULTI on the shelf (Overdrive x5, Flense-Claw x5, Sky-Rack x4, Turbine x4), watch that the auto-lowest-HP break-race doesn't strictly out-tempo aimed SINGLE burst. The min-1 damage floor + DEF blunting each of many small hits is the intended check; if MULTI still wins the disable-race too reliably, raise per-hit mana cost or shave a hit. (3) CORE-SNIPE HEADS/ARMS. Oracle Lens (p7), Meteor (p9) and Sky-Rack can aim the core (can_target_core=true). The §13.3 AI gate keeps ordinary BATTLE enemies off your core, and only the elite/boss loadouts here carry core-targeters, so player-side these are strong offense and enemy-side they are correctly reserved for DEATH-stakes nodes - but confirm the AI honors that gate. (4) PART_RESTORE FEEL. Restore can never un-break a disabled part, so it must feel worth a whole turn as proactive keep-alive; if it feels dead, players will just run DEF_BUFF everywhere and the mend families (cobble_sons, pith_sinew) go unused. Deep-Pulse Sac (g8) on the boss is the stress test - a self-sustaining opponent should feel like a puzzle, not a stalemate. (5) FEATHERWEIGHT ARCHETYPE. Pocketful bits (w3-6) + light cores let a build pay almost no weight tax; the glass HP (mostly 6-8, cores 24-30) is the intended counter. Watch that a feather build isn't strictly better than balanced once part-targeting punishes its low HP. (6) GRINLET'S PAW (p9, w5, hp6). A 9-power SINGLE on a weightless arm is the sharpest glass-cannon in the set; if its hp6 doesn't get punished fast enough it may be a must-pick - first place to nerf is its HP down to reinforce 'breaks if looked at.'
