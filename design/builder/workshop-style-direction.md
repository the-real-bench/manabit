# Workshop Style Direction - the beauty redesign

**STATUS: PROPOSED - awaiting owner sign-off (2026-07-18). No implementation until signed.**

Product of the owner-ordered 6-lane style-research expedition (workflow wf_ab828f57-15f, killed by a session restart, continued and completed as wf_0a1c3dbd-d77). 6/6 lanes reported, 89 real screenshots viewed. Games studied: Gundam Breaker 4, Custom Robo (GC), Medabots (GBA), Mega Man Battle Network, Armored Core 6, Daemon X Machina, Backpack Battles, Stacklands, Balatro, Wildfrost, Unpacking, Cult of the Lamb.

## Diagnosis - why the current Workshop reads "terrible"

1. **Leader lines are the #1 debug tell:** six thin lines radiate from floating label-plates and literally cross the toy's body - it reads as an engineering callout diagram or a Godot gizmo overlay. Not one of the eight reference games uses a single callout line anywhere.
2. **The stage is a hard-edged flat teal rectangle** butted into the layout like a viewport: dead margins, no lamplight pool, no rim on the felt, no visible workshop wall or props. A render target, not a display stand.
3. **The toy is not the hero:** it sits small and centered, visually smaller than its own Ledger panel, with no hero angle, no dramatic key light, and a weak contact shadow. GB4/AC6/Medabots all prove premium feel is 90% staging the machine big, lit, and grounded.
4. **Box soup:** the Ledger, six plates, five buttons, tray cards, and nav chips are all the same brown rounded-rect with the same thin 1px tan border on a flat fill - zero material story (no grain, felt weave, parchment, stitching, brass), zero shadows.
5. **Stats are letter-code strings** ('A1 D2 S1 - 12 wt', 'ATK 4 - WT 26 - MULTI') - abbreviation soup that mirrors AC6 mil-fiction codenames without the fiction excuse. Every reference converts stats to icon + pip + colored token.
6. **The Balance is a flat yellow progress bar** with raw red error text - engine-default voice, the opposite of a crafted brass instrument.
7. **Five identical rectangular buttons side by side** (BIND/SPAR/BOUT/VENTURE/BOX) - the signature of debug UI; no reference ever shows two same-shaped rectangles adjacent, and there is no single hero action.
8. **Everything is pixel-aligned to a rigid grid** with even spacing, no rotation, no overlap, no shadow - reads spreadsheet/inspector, not handmade objects on a bench.
9. **The tray is a row of uniform dark card wells** that read as empty sockets or debug placeholders rather than owned treasures.

## The direction (one paragraph)

The Workshop becomes ONE room, not five panels: a toymaker's bench under a warm lamp, where every UI surface is a nameable physical object and the Manabit is the lit hero at its center. Structure is stolen from the mech garages - the toy staged big at a slight low hero angle on a felt-topped display stand with a brass rim, a single crafted slot rail instead of floating callouts, selection shown by glowing the part on the toy itself, and instant hover-delta stat previews - but every surface is re-rendered in MANABIT's existing walnut, brass, parchment, and felt tokens with the cozy games' material grammar: frame + inset + brass accent + one shared soft-shadow direction on every container, stats as icon-and-pip charms instead of letter codes, empty states as die-cut promises instead of gray boxes, exactly one hero-shaped action (the bind seal), and ornament concentrated into tiny repeated details (a rivet, a stitch, a ruled line, a wax dot) with slight handmade rotation. Color stays Balatro-disciplined - four or five accent hues locked to meanings on the warm neutral ground, saturation reserved for the toy and celebration - so the screen reads rich, warm, and crafted: a bench you tinker at, never a hangar and never a casino.

## Concrete moves (ordered, all buildable in code-built Godot UI)

1. **DELETE the six perimeter plates and every leader line.** Replace with a single crafted slot rail: six brass socket medallions (etched slot glyph, Baloo 2 microlabel, ~88px targets per DESIGN.md 3) docked in an arc along the stage rim (GB4 tab rail structure + CotL socket ring). Empty medallion = die-cut darker silhouette of the bit shape (never gray); filled = tiny bit icon + rarity rim material; active = warm-lit with --brass-hi #E8C87E; amber ! pip for needed slots survives from SlotField spec. Pure StyleBoxFlat + icon work, keeps the existing tap-socket-to-filter behavior 1:1.
2. **Stage as display stand, not viewport:** round the felt-teal rect's corners, draw a stitched/brass rim border, pool a soft radial --lamp-key gradient behind the toy, and let a warm walnut workshop wall (low-contrast woodgrain via warmth.gd) show around all four edges so the stage never touches screen edge (Stacklands play mat + CR shadow box). Reframe the camera so the toy stands 70-80% of stage height at the existing 18-deg hero yaw, slightly low angle, strong contact shadow - ManabitStage already has the FOV-50 auto-framing, shelf line, rim light, and shadow plumbing.
3. **Selection glows ON the toy** (AC6 part-outline translated): hovering a tray card or medallion rim-lights that part of the Manabit in --glow-base amber (or the core's affinity tint), and the matching medallion warms - the toy itself becomes the link between tray and slots, using the existing --socket-flare 200ms token.
4. **One material-sandwich StyleBox recipe applied to EVERY container** (Balatro/Wildfrost layered-paper trick): walnut frame + parchment or panel-deep inset + one brass accent detail (rivet, clip, stitched tab) + a single shared shadow offset direction. Add tokens to DESIGN.md: --shadow-soft (offset 0/3px, dark walnut 40%), --radius-card 10, --stitch-detail. This retrofits Ledger, tray, nav, and cards without moving a single element - the cheapest global anti-debug fix.
5. **The Ledger becomes a parchment spec card with a brass clip:** each stat = slot icon + name in Baloo 2 + a segmented brass-pip lozenge (CR pips + Medabots battery gauge) colored by the existing --stat-atk/def/spd/energy tokens, number in a circular end-cap. Hover or drag any bit = instant whole-column preview 'ATK 15 -> 19' with stitched up/down arrows in --delta-pos leaf / --delta-neg ember (AC6 current>>new grammar + DXM zero-click preview) - StatDeltaRow's projecting state already carries the data.
6. **The Balance becomes a drawn brass steelyard scale:** a custom-draw beam that visibly tips as weight grows, needle sweeping into a red-wax overload zone (--wax-seal #B4472E), keeping the honest 'SPD x -> y (-N)' math but rewording status copy in-world ('she'll wheeze - heavy in the legs') - WeightMeter states map 1:1, and it turns the current yellow debug bar into the most fantasy-selling object on screen.
7. **One hero action only:** BIND MANABIT becomes a stamped brass plate with a wax-seal press motif (extends the existing wax-seal/hold vocabulary); SPAR / BOUT / VENTURE / BOX OF SCRAP demote to quiet ink-and-underline links or small hanging wooden signs (Unpacking pause menu + MMBN's lone RUN!). Kills the five-identical-rectangles row outright.
8. **Bit cards adopt a locked specimen anatomy** (Stacklands + CotL): pastel slot-color title strip per socket family (add 5 pastel family tokens to DESIGN.md 1), existing icon in a soft circular color blob, weight and headline stat as small corner blobs with icons (never 'ATK 4 - WT 26' strings), rarity as the already-tokened frame material (tin/brass/runed-wood) plus a small stamped seal, wax dot = seated, paper stamp = NEW, dupes as peeking stacked strips - identical placement on all 80 bits for effortless scanning.
9. **The tray becomes a felt-lined wooden parts drawer** the cards sit IN: carved bracket corners, dovetail edge detail, cards resting with 1-2 deg random rotation and soft shadows (Wildfrost tray + BPB pouches); the TrayToolbar chips become small skewed file-tabs on the drawer lip. Same layout box, new furniture identity.
10. **Dim-and-focus inspect** (Wildfrost Bumbo pattern) replaces the informational job the plates were doing: tapping a socket or long-pressing a bit dims the room ~60% warm (never black), scales the toy toward camera, and spawns 2-4 rounded parchment chips near that part (name, icon+stat tokens, one flavor line) - progressive disclosure, no simultaneous clutter, no lines.
11. **Small parchment anatomy inset** (CR body-silhouette / gunpla manual figure): a card near the slot rail showing the Manabit outline with six circled socket letters, the active socket inked in - sells the model-kit fantasy, gives drag-and-drop a legend, costs one static texture plus a highlight state.
12. **Kill all flat negative space:** low-contrast woodgrain/stitched-felt wash across the background via warmth.gd, a faint blueprint-style Manabit silhouette watermark behind the stage (Medabots honeycomb lesson), plus 3-4 static corner props (thread spool, screwdriver, pencil, tea-ring stain) confined to margins (Unpacking desk garnish) - cheap sprites, huge fantasy payoff.
13. **Lock the semantic color economy in DESIGN.md as a RULE:** ember=ATK, slate=DEF, leaf=SPD, teal=mana/Glimmer, brass-gold=selection/celebration only - one hue never means two things, gold and glow rationed to the selected object and win moments (Balatro discipline, and the explicit anti-casino guard). Ban letter-code stat strings project-wide; every stat everywhere is icon + colored capsule.
14. **Typography and jitter pass:** Baloo 2 display text always carries a 1-2px warm dark shade (never naked on the background), microlabels in small caps, and celebration moments use restraint - letterbox warm dim + soft godrays + one enlarged object (Wildfrost unlock), reusing the existing RarityBurst tiers, no confetti storms.
